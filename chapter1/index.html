My favorite drink ever was a Lotus energy drink from the small drive-thru coffee huts in Fairbanks, Alaska. These huts are everywhere up there, and while they also make some of the best coffee around, what really stood out to me were their energy drinks. Instead of just making regular coffee, they mix Lotus energy concentrate or Red Bull with flavored soda syrups poured over ice to create bright, colorful combinations. I loved how customizable they were, since every hut had its own creative flavor combos to choose from. They were sweet, fizzy, and gave a smooth energy boost without tasting like a typical energy drink. I would grab one whenever I needed a pick-me-up while working and helping my friend on their dry cabin project out in North Pole. I would definitely recommend trying one if you're ever in Alaska.
