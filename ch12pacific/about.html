Our Story

Pacific Trails Resort began, as the most enduring things often do, with a family and a dream. Long before there was a resort, there was a deep love for the coast — for the way the morning light settled over the water, for the quiet strength of the trees, for the stillness that seems to remind a person who they are. That love became a vision: a place where others could come not only to visit, but to pause, breathe, and feel restored.

The owners imagined more than a beautiful destination. They hoped to create a sanctuary shaped by faith, family, and gratitude, where every detail would reflect care and purpose. In time, that dream took form in peaceful yurts, winding trails, and welcoming spaces designed to offer comfort without distraction and beauty without excess. Nothing was added carelessly; everything was chosen to feel intentional, calm, and deeply human.

What makes Pacific Trails Resort special is not only its setting, but the spirit behind it. It carries the warmth of a family business, the tenderness of a place built with love, and the quiet dignity of something created to be shared. Guests arrive seeking rest, but many leave with something more lasting: a sense of peace, a softened heart, and the feeling that they have been somewhere meaningful.

At its heart, Pacific Trails Resort is a reflection of what the family believed from the beginning — that when a place is built with faith, guided by love, and shaped with a sincere desire to care for others, it can become more than a resort. It can become a memory, a refuge, and a reminder that beauty, when shared, has the power to heal.
